Fun Facts about the iris

Did you know…?

👁

79% of people have brown eyes — by far the most common iris colour on Earth.

🧬

Blue eyes are a genetic mutation — everyone with blue eyes shares a common ancestor who lived around 6,000–10,000 years ago.

💎

Grey eyes are rare — they occur mainly in people of Eastern European origin and contain less melanin than blue eyes.

☀️

Eye colour affects light sensitivity — darker eyes adapt more quickly to sudden changes in lighting.

🔬

Every iris is absolutely unique — no two people share identical iris patterns, not even identical twins.

🎨

The iris changes with age — colour and patterns can evolve, especially in the first years of life.
